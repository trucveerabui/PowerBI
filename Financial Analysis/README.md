# Financial Dashboard

This Financial Dashboard is designed to provide a comprehensive view of revenue performance against forecast in 2024 and Q1 2025. By consolidating data across products, segments, and geographies, the dashboard enables leaders to monitor financial health, identify performance gaps, and uncover key revenue drivers. It supports data-driven decision-making by highlighting trends, variances, and areas requiring strategic focus.


- Revenue: Revenue is consistently below forecast, indicating ongoing gaps between planned and actual performance. This highlights the need to reassess forecasting assumptions and strengthen execution to improve forecast accuracy.
- Product Performance: Top products (Paseo, VTT, Velo) contribute ~55–60% of total revenue. Paseo is consistently the largest contributor (~27–29%). Most products are under forecast, Amarilla is a positive outlier.
- Segment Performance: Government is the dominant segment. Small Business is the second-largest contributor. Enterprise, Midmarket, and Channel Partners contribute relatively little.
- Geography Performance: Revenue is well distributed across top 5 countries (US, Canada, France, Germany, Mexico).
