# CRM Dashboard

This dashboard helps track total leads, closed deals, conversion rates, and deal values across different products, countries, industries and organization sizes. 

- Overall Performance: The company has a healthy pipeline with a substantial number of leads, but only 12% are converted to deals, indicating room for improvement. 
- Product Performance: SaaS leads in popularity, while Services leads in deal value. Conversion rates are balanced across products.
- Monthly Trends: Closed deal values increased early in the year, likely due to effective sales efforts or favourable market conditions, but later decreased, potentially due to seasonal factors.
- Geographical Performance: The Netherlands has the highest conversion rate, suggesting effective localised strategies. Germany’s lower rate indicates potential for enhancement.
- Sales Agent Performance: Laura Thompson excels with the highest closed leads and closed deal values, highlighting her effectiveness.
